The rules it keeps

Her name never leaves your phone.
Every entry is read before it goes anywhere, and any name in it is swapped for a handle — "the one who ordered the whole fish", "the girl from the bookstore". She didn't sign up for this.
Nothing is public, ever.
There is no feed, no profile, no upvotes and no browsing. Entries go to one group chat that you assembled, and nowhere else.
Nobody joins without joining.
No contact list is read, no number is imported. Your circle is whoever is already in the group chat you added the bot to.
Your friends read it the day you write it.
Nothing is held back from the room. Journaling is good; journaling to the handful of people who'll be honest with you is better, and a group chat nobody can talk in isn't one.
One update, and it's yours to give.
Two weeks on it asks you what happened — privately, not the room. Long enough that you've already decided, so nobody talked you out of it. Then your words go in, and that entry is closed.
Leaving is one word.
Send STOP and you're out and forgotten. Or remove the bot from the group, and it stops.
